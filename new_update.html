01 / ABOUT

Understanding how systems are built — and how they break.

I’m a cybersecurity undergraduate at Al-Hikmah University with a growing focus on cloud security. My path so far has taken me through TryHackMe and Cisco NetAcad — covering the CIA Triad, cryptography (symmetric/asymmetric, hashing, digital signatures), malware analysis, social engineering, and network attack vectors like MitM and DDoS.

Alongside the security side, I’m building solid frontend fundamentals (HTML, CSS, JavaScript, React, Node.js) because understanding how systems are built makes me better at knowing how they break.

I take notes, not shortcuts — everything I learn gets written up in my own words before it counts as “known.”
